## Option 2 — on your own computer

Download and unpack the project:

```bash
curl -L https://github.com/HocheggerLab/tcga-capstone-2026/archive/refs/heads/main.zip -o capstone.zip
unzip capstone.zip
cd tcga-capstone-2026-main
uv sync
code .
```

`uv sync` installs exactly the packages the notebooks need into a private
`.venv`. In VS Code, open a notebook, click **Select Kernel** →
**Python Environments** → `.venv`, and run it from the top.

All your data is already in `data/`, so this works offline.

## About the data

This is **real, de-identified patient data**. The barcodes are public
pseudonyms, not names. Treat it with the respect you would give your
own medical record: never try to work out who a patient is, and do not
combine it with anything that could.

All of it comes from the open-access tier of two public projects:

- **The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).** The results shown here are in
  whole or part based upon data generated by the TCGA Research Network:
  https://www.cancer.gov/tcga.
- **The Genotype-Tissue Expression (GTEx) Project**, which supplies the
  normal tissue. GTEx was supported by the Common Fund of the Office of
  the Director of the National Institutes of Health, and by NCI, NHGRI,
  NHLBI, NIDA, NIMH and NINDS.

It was processed by others before it reached you, and their work should
be cited whenever you use it:

- Expression - Vivian J *et al.* Toil enables reproducible, open source,
  big biomedical data analyses. *Nat Biotechnol* **35**, 314-316 (2017).
- Access - Goldman MJ *et al.* Visualizing and interpreting cancer
  genomics data via the Xena platform. *Nat Biotechnol* **38**, 675-678
  (2020).
- TP53 mutation calls - Ellrott K *et al.* Scalable open science approach
  for mutation calling of tumor exomes using multiple genomic pipelines.
  *Cell Syst* **6**, 271-281 (2018).
- Copy number - Hoadley KA *et al.* Cell-of-origin patterns dominate the
  molecular classification of 10,000 tumors from 33 types of cancer.
  *Cell* **173**, 291-304 (2018).
- Clinical follow-up - Liu J *et al.* An integrated TCGA pan-cancer
  clinical data resource to drive high-quality survival outcome
  analytics. *Cell* **173**, 400-416 (2018).
